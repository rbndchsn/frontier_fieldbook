# RDR2 — Spoiler-free field guide

## Setup
- Steam Ultimate Edition, Story Mode, Highball on M4 Max MacBook Pro (40-core GPU, 128 GB memory).
- Xbox wireless controller; pair in macOS Bluetooth settings. Connect TV by HDMI and enable TV Game Mode.
- Start at 1440p or 1080p, high graphics, then tune for stable performance. Do not assume every graphics option works through Highball.

## Getting started
Chapter 1 is a guided introduction. Chapter 2 opens free roaming; complete early story missions to unlock stables, hunting-related features and fishing. Early Chapter 3 is another optional base for long-term exploration. There is no need to rush. Story missions sometimes require combat and prescribed actions.

## Wilderness life
Ride slowly, study animals with binoculars, collect plants, investigate unfamiliar landmarks and use the in-game journal and compendium. Avoid online maps if you want discoveries to remain surprises. Establish temporary camps where permitted; cook meat and rest. Keep food and horse provisions available. Hunt selectively and sell meat, pelts and carcasses to suitable merchants; trappers also craft from eligible pelts. Carcasses can spoil. Three-star animals and appropriate in-game weapons help preserve pelt quality. Use the compendium to learn suitable equipment.

## Towns and people
Greet strangers, help when opportunities arise, trade with merchants and explore without rushing. Honor affects prices and some story outcomes. Not all encounters are friendly; retreat when possible.

## Horse care
Feed, brush, calm and ride your horse to build bonding. Keep horse reviver available when possible; horses can die permanently. Stables allow you to manage owned horses and tack once unlocked.

## Save and return
Pause > Story > Save Game. Keep autosave enabled, and maintain a separate manual save at the start of a preferred chapter. Save before risky adventures when the game permits. Save slots do not merge progress.
