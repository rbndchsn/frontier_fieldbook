# RDR2 — The independent frontier naturalist

## Objective
Enjoy a living wilderness, not a second job. Prioritize discovery, horses, ecological observation, financial independence and helping strangers. Use force only when necessary for survival or required story objectives. Avoid cheats and spoilers.

## Progression
1. Finish Chapter 1 and learn controls.
2. Use Chapter 2 to unlock stables, fishing and key equipment; explore at leisure.
3. Optionally reach early Chapter 3 and establish a protected manual save.
4. Maintain a separate active wilderness save and, if desired, a separate story-progression save. Do not overwrite the protected save.

## Economic loop
Explore > hunt selectively / gather / fish > trade > restock > return to wilderness. Treasure hunting and paid missions can supplement income. Avoid needless ammunition costs, bounties and dangerous encounters. Hunting is a viable leisure activity but not necessarily the fastest route to wealth. Keep enough cash and supplies for emergencies; no arbitrary wealth target is required.

## Personal code
Help people when practical; avoid civilian harm; prefer peaceful resolutions; respect your horse; use animal products rather than hunting indiscriminately. Honor is a flexible game mechanic, not a requirement to behave perfectly.

## Minimal maintenance
Eat and rest as needed, care for your horse, maintain weapons, and replenish supplies. Camp upgrades and chores are optional for this play style. Stop whenever you want; story progress can wait.

## Optional experiment
Compare two independent saves by cash, honor, discoveries and time played. Only track what is enjoyable; the game should not become a spreadsheet.
